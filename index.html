Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NexGen training programs, certifications, and delivery formats.

What makes NexGen C2C Skills unique compared to other online courses?

NexGen combines 100% hands-on AI tools practice with globally celebrated corporate mindset frameworks (such as The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People and Atomic Habits) along with authentic shop-floor technical engineering (PLC, Lean, Robotics). You don't just learn concepts; you build measurable workplace capabilities.

Do I need a technical coding background to join the AI Foundation course?

No prior coding experience is required for the Foundation and Accelerator tracks! We focus on prompt crafting, no-code AI tools, everyday productivity, research, writing, and decision automation accessible to all students and professionals.

What certifications will I receive upon completing a program?

You will receive an Industry-Recognized NexGen C2C Certificate of Completion detailing your capstone projects, specific AI tools mastered, and hours of hands-on practical training to showcase directly on LinkedIn and your resume.

Can NexGen conduct customized workshops for our college or company?

Yes! We specialize in tailored institutional training for colleges (Faculty Development Programs, student finishing schools) and corporate organizations (custom AI adoption, Lean manufacturing, and PLC automation batches).
